TL;DR: JLT predicts the clean VAE endpoint and converts it to velocity with a fixed affine readout. On class-conditional ImageNet 256×256, matched clean-latent prediction improves FID-50K from 6.56 to 2.84 at Base, from 2.12 to 1.83 at Large, and from 1.60 to 1.19 at Huge.

The solver interface need not be the neural interface. (a) Direct velocity prediction learns the vector field internally; clean-endpoint prediction exposes the clean latent and shifts the residual response to an exact readout. (b) Under matched ImageNet 256×256 setups, this reparameterization improves FID across B/1, L/1, and H/1.

Matched Base-scale samples. Random class-conditional generations from B/1 models under the same 50-step Heun sampler and CFG 2.9. Clean prediction keeps global structure and detail; direct velocity prediction more often distorts or blurs.

Loss Weighting

Holding the clean output fixed, velocity-space MSE induces a (1-t)-2 weight on the clean error. Replacing it with unweighted clean MSE does not remove the gain.

Method

Prediction Targets

Images are encoded by a frozen VAE. The linear path is z_t = t * x + (1-t) * epsilon, with sample-wise velocity v = x - epsilon. A clean prediction converts to the same velocity by

v_hat = (x_hat - z_t) / (1 - t)

For squared error, the optimal clean and velocity predictors are algebraically equivalent. A finite Transformer is not: predicting v has to represent the input residual and time-dependent gain internally, while predicting x leaves that response to the readout.

Target Geometry

Under a local Gaussian model, velocity adds a unit floor to every latent direction. Measured FLUX.2 channel spectra match that shift.

Other Checks

  1. Qwen-VAE, matched B/1: The same clean-versus-velocity comparison at CFG 2.9 ends at FID 6.54 versus 6.85 after 250K steps. Clean prediction is lower at 150K, 200K, and 250K.
  2. CFG sweeps: JLT-B/1 FID falls through the grid and is 2.70 at CFG 3.0. JLT-H/1 FID is lowest at 1.19 when CFG is 2.2.
  3. Loss weighting: Unweighted clean MSE at Base finishes at FID 2.38 and IS 256.88, so the gain is not only the induced time weight.
